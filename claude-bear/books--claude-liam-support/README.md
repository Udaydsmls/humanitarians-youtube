# Claude, On Call — The Support Plugin

If you have customers, you have support, and it doesn't wait for business
hours. The natural guess is that Claude just answers tickets on its own —
but hand it a customer whose payment has failed three times, angry after
two loyal years, and you can see why it never sends on autopilot. It
drafts instead: triaging the queue into now/later/known, writing a reply
from your tone and knowledge base, flagging the customers who need a
human now, and turning the questions you keep answering into a published
FAQ. Watch the angry-customer draft come back around — Claude catches the
tone, you add the one line only you would know to add, and it's you who
sends it. The same review rule that never relaxes for a live reply flips
for a published FAQ answer: reviewed once, it serves every future
customer without you touching it again.
