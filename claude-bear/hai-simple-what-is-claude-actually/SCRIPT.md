## S01 — Stakes

You've used Claude. Typed something in, got something useful back. But if someone
asked you what it actually is, you'd hesitate.

---

## S02 — Wrong Guess (planted)

The natural answer is: a very smart search engine. You type, something finds a good
answer, surfaces it.

---

## S03 — ANCHOR PLANTED

*(THE ANCHOR. This exact visual returns at S09.)*

Hold on to one example: ask what the capital of France is. The answer comes back
right. That feels exactly like retrieval.

---

## S04 — Break the Wrong Guess

But a search engine reads from an index. Rephrase the question twenty different ways —
Claude never misses. There is no index.

---

## S05 — Mechanism (part 1)

Claude is a neural network trained on a huge amount of text to do one thing: given
these words, predict which words come next.

---

## S06 — Mechanism (part 2)

A second training step shaped those predictions toward helpful, honest, and harmless
responses — not by layering rules on top, but by adjusting the same weights.

---

## S07 — Mechanism (part 3)

So when you type, Claude doesn't look anything up. It generates a response: the
continuation that its training says belongs next, here, in this conversation.

---

## S08 — ONE FLAG

*(The reel's only hedge — labelled as one.)*

One flag: what Claude learned has a cutoff date. And in its base form it has no live
web access. Anything after the cutoff, you'd need to supply.

---

## S09 — ANCHOR PAYOFF

*(THE ANCHOR RETURNS — same composition as S03.)*

Back to the capital of France. Claude answers not by reading a database entry, but
because that answer appeared in millions of training examples. It's in the weights.

---

## S10 — Both Directions (A)

Here's why this helps: the same mechanism — predict useful next words — is what lets
Claude write code, translate languages, summarize. One machine, many things.

---

## S11 — Both Directions (B)

And why it doesn't: the same mechanism explains confident wrong answers. Claude
generates plausible text. Plausible and verified are not the same thing.

---

## BCRY — Carry-Out (Remotion)

Claude isn't finding answers — it's generating the next most useful thing to say,
shaped by everything Anthropic taught it to care about.
