# Claude, Equipped — What a Cowork Plugin Actually Is

Installing a Cowork plugin doesn't teach Claude more facts — it hands Claude
equipment. Four parts can ship inside one: skills that switch on when
they're relevant, connectors that reach into your CRM or spreadsheets,
slash-commands that shortcut a whole job, and subagents that quietly
coordinate multi-step work. That's why it's nothing like a smarter prompt —
a prompt can't log into your tools, add a shortcut to your menu, or hand
off a job to a coordinator. Watch the same distinction return at the end:
the difference between asking a well-read friend what to watch for in a
contract, and asking someone who reviews contracts for a living. Plugins
are open source (free, transparent, community-maintained, and yours to
adjust), and they get dramatically more powerful once you configure them
for your actual process. But four limits hold no matter what: no perfect
judgment, no new knowledge, only your existing access, and it's a tool —
not a replacement. The consequential calls stay yours.

## Deliberately not claimed

No plugin count, no roster, and no prices — the video says "an official
collection plus community additions" and "beyond your paid plan," never a
specific number or price, since counts and prices move faster than a video
can. No named third-party tool beyond the generic "your CRM" / "your
spreadsheets," since the mechanism (connect, then reach further) holds
regardless of which one you use. No claim about how long customizing takes
beyond illustrative framing — not a timed guarantee.
