# Claude, On Message — The Marketing Plugin

You install the marketing plugin expecting a faster way to get content
written. That's not what changes. Claude stops being a content vending
machine and becomes a collaborator that plans campaigns, reads your
competitors, and interprets your performance data with you — five sides
under one install: content, campaigns, competitors, performance, brand.
The real lever isn't the install, though — it's fifteen minutes of setup:
your brand voice calibrated from a few paragraphs you've actually written,
your audience described as narrowly as you can stand ("small-business
owners considering AI tools but not technical" beats "professionals" every
time), and what you sell. That's the difference between a first draft
that's fifty-percent right and one that's eighty-percent right. Watch it
build a content calendar, draft a five-email launch sequence, repurpose
one post into five formats, and read a month of metrics for what to do
next — all of it edited, not invented from a blank page. The one honest
limit: it drafts well, but review is still yours, and it won't replace
your CMO.

## YOUR TURN

I just installed the marketing plugin. Before drafting anything, interview
me: (1) ask me to paste two or three paragraphs I've written so you can
learn my brand voice, (2) ask who my audience is and push me to get
specific, (3) ask what I sell and what makes it different. Then summarize
my brand voice, audience, and positioning back to me as a short config I
can save — and name the first campaign we should build together.

Run that today, on your own brand — not the video's example.
