## Description

Can Claude's legal and finance plugins screen your legal and financial work for you? Not
quite — one flags the risk in a contract, the other models the scenario in your numbers,
and both stop well short of being your lawyer or your accountant.

From Humanitarians AI: short, plain explanations of how Claude actually works, for a
general audience meeting Claude for the first time. Liam, in for Professor Bear.

In this one: the shared pattern behind both plugins (screen the risk, or accelerate the
analysis); how the legal plugin reads a contract and sorts issues into green, yellow, and
red; exactly where it stops — no legal advice, no counsel for big deals; how the finance
plugin turns burn rate, runway, and best/likely/worst scenario modeling into something you
actually run instead of postpone; exactly where it stops — no tax filing, no bookkeeping;
and the habits (routine, not occasional; organized documents) that make either one worth
using at all.

Try it yourself — the video ends with a paste-ready prompt for screening your own next
contract and budget decision in Claude today.
