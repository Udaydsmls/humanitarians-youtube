**The wrong guess it defeats:** that the first move is a *policy* — a

a contract-review tool one team started using without telling anyone) slips
past a policy every time, because the policy never knew it existed. The
inventory is what catches it — a maintained list of what's actually
running, not a rule about what's supposed to be running.
