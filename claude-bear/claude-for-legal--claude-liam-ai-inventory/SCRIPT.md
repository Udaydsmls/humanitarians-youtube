## Deliberately not claimed

- **Not that a policy is useless.** The reel never says skip the policy —
  only that it has to follow the inventory, not precede it, or it governs
  nothing real.
- **Not a specific Claude product feature.** Because the source sheet's
  actual facts were never written (see QUESTION.md), this script describes
  the generic mechanics of an AI-inventory governance practice rather than
  citing any particular tool's UI or output format.
- **No accusation that anyone was negligent.** A quietly-adopted tool is
  presented as an ordinary gap in an informal process, not a failure by any
  named person or team.

## Handoff prompt (BHTF, read aloud)

> "List every AI tool your team used this month — drafting help, research
> search, document review, anything. For each one, write down what it does,
> what data goes through it, and who owns it."

Why it's worth running: the exercise surfaces the same gap the reel is
built around — any row where "owner" comes up blank is a tool that a policy
alone would never have caught.
