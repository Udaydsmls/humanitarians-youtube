# Claude, Configured — Installing and Customizing Plugins

You click install on a Cowork plugin and it's ready in seconds — but that
first version is running on generic defaults, not on your business. Click
customize and it doesn't hand you a settings panel; it starts a plain
conversation, asking what you do, who your customers are, your voice, your
formats. Watch one real example: a web design studio tells a marketing
plugin about its professional-services clients, and the same request that
used to come back generic — a content calendar, a competitor scan — comes
back built for that studio specifically. Some plugins go further still,
connecting to your CRM or your live database so they work with your actual
numbers instead of what you hand them. Disable a plugin and its
customization waits, untouched, for whenever you turn it back on — the
only catch is that all of it lives on your machine, so a second computer
starts from zero.

## Deliberately not claimed

No plugin count, no roster, no prices, and no platform list — the video
says "an official collection plus community additions" and "on your
machine," never a specific number or price, since counts and prices move
faster than a video can. No named third-party tool beyond the generic
"your CRM" / "your live database," since the mechanism (connect, then
reach further) holds regardless of which one you use. No claim about how
long customizing takes beyond "a few minutes" — illustrative, not a
timed guarantee.
