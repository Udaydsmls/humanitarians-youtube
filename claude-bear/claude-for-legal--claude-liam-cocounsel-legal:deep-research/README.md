# Claude, Cocounsel Legal: Deep Research.

Claude picked up a skill called deep-research — did it actually go off and
research the law using its own legal judgment? No. A "skill" is a folder
holding one file, SKILL.md, that Claude reads before it starts and then
follows step by step, in order. Delete the folder and Claude loses no legal
reasoning, because there was nothing added — it just stops following that
one procedure. That makes a skill a specification, not a new judgment: the
payoff is the same search-and-cite process, every question — legal research
and synthesis via Westlaw Deep Research; the limit is anything the file
never wrote down. And watching Claude produce a well-cited memo, or miss a
case, proves nothing on its own about whether it actually exercised
judgment.
