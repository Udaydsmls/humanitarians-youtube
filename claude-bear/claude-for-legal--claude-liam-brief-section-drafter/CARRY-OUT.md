**Carry-out line (BCRY, read before any other narration — CARRY-OUT LAW):**

> Same spec in, same section out, every time. Checking the facts, the cases, and anything
> outside that spec is still your job.

**Test:** repeated cold, out of context, next week — still true, still specific to this
skill (names the actual behavior: reliable inside the spec, unchecked outside it), not a
generic "AI can write things" line. It compresses the distinction that matters (repeatable
drafting vs. who verifies it), not the topic (brief-section-drafter itself).

**Wrong guess it defeats:** that "Claude can draft a brief section" means the section
arrives finished — citations solid, facts settled, argument airtight, nothing left to
check. The reel's B03 states the skill's actual constraint (house style, case theory,
every fact cited, every case checked, every argument tied to the theory) and stops there
without judging whether that's a good or bad design; the carry-out is what a viewer should
keep: the skill's reliability is scoped to the spec, and everything outside the spec —
verifying facts and case law — is still the lawyer's job.

("Same input, same output, every run. Know the limit: only what the file says.") — reused
as this video's single global carry-out. The redo's only change is which beat carries it
