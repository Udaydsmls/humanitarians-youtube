## Description

Do you have to be a professional salesperson with a CRM to use Claude's sales plugin? No —
it meets you where you are. It researches a prospect, preps you for a call, drafts the
follow-up, and helps you see where your pipeline actually stands. It never has the
conversation for you: the relationship stays yours.

From Humanitarians AI: short, plain explanations of how Claude actually works, for a
general audience meeting Claude for the first time. Liam, in for Professor Bear.

In this one: who the plugin is actually built for (consultants, freelancers, agency
owners — not a sales team with an administrator); the five kinds of help it gives
(research, prep, follow-up, pipeline, outreach); a full walkthrough of a freelancer
prepping for, then following up after, a discovery call with an agency they barely know;
how it helps you see a scattered prospect list as an ordered pipeline; and both directions
— what it's good at, and the one thing it should never be asked to replace: actually being
present in the conversation.

Try it yourself — the video ends with a paste-ready prompt for prepping your own next
client conversation in Claude today.
