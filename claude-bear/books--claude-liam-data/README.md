# Claude, By the Numbers — The Data Plugin

You've got a year of revenue sitting in a spreadsheet you never open —
because opening it means formulas, and formulas mean guessing at syntax
and still not trusting the answer. It doesn't have to. Point Claude at
that same file and ask the way you'd ask a colleague, and it explores,
cleans, visualizes, and compares the numbers back to you in plain
sentences: which clients bring in the most, whether the mix has shifted,
whether you're actually profitable month to month. Turn it into a
five-minute monthly ritual and you catch a client sliding — or a cost
climbing — while there's still time to do something about it. The one
honest catch: the read is only as good as the data you feed it and the
assumptions underneath, so the plugin surfaces the number and you keep
the judgment.

## YOUR TURN

I run a small business. Here's a spreadsheet of my invoices and one of my
expenses for the year: tell me what's in each file and flag anything
messy, then give me a business-health check — am I profitable month to
month, which months were strongest and weakest, is any expense growing
faster than revenue — and end with the one follow-up question I should
ask next.

Run that today, on your own invoices and expenses, not the video's example.

## Deliberately not claimed

No plugin name/version number and no specific spreadsheet template — "a
folder called Finances," "invoices and expenses" stand in for whatever
files you actually keep. No claim that the plugin replaces an accountant
or financial advisor: the reel is explicit that clean inputs and sound
assumptions are still on you, and that the plugin surfaces a number, not
a decision — whether to call a client, discount, or let them go stays
yours to judge.
