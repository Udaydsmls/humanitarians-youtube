## Deliberately not claimed

- **Not that documentation doesn't matter.** The reel never says skip the
  notes — only that the conflict check has to come before them, or the
  notes themselves become the liability.
- **Not a specific Claude product feature.** Because the source sheet's
  actual facts were never written (see QUESTION.md), this script describes
  the generic mechanics of a legal-intake conflict check rather than citing
  any particular tool's UI or output format.
- **No accusation that anyone was negligent.** A client whose story runs
  ahead of the conflict question is presented as the ordinary shape of a
  conversation, not a failure by any named person or firm.

## Handoff prompt (BHTF, read aloud)

> "I'm about to take an intake call with a new client. Before I let them
> tell me the whole story, help me write a short list of questions I need
> answered first — starting with who's on the other side — so I catch a
> conflict before I've heard anything I can't unhear."

Why it's worth running: the exercise forces the same reordering the reel is
built around — putting the one gatekeeping question ahead of the story
instead of after it.
