# Three You Can Take Back. One You Can't.

You type one detail into an AI — a partner, a hobby, a city — and assume
there's a single answer to "where does that go." There isn't. It lands in
four different places, and they are not the same kind of place: the
conversation itself, your account's memory file, the provider's operational
systems, and — the one that doesn't reverse — model training. This walks the
four, level by level, ranked on the axis that actually matters: not secrecy,
reversibility.

## Deliberately not claimed

Not naming a specific product's retention period — the reel says "usually
written down," never a number of days, because that varies by provider.
Not asserting every provider trains on conversations — B16/B19 both hedge
with "some providers" / "where providers train." No verdict on whether any
provider's retention design is good policy — explaining the mechanism (a
clock you don't set) is not the same as ruling on whether that's the right
way to run a service.
