# Carry-Out

## The Carry-Out Sentence
"In a million-dimensional space, an invisible nudge on every coordinate accumulates into a decisive shift across the whole model."

## Wrong Guess Defeated
"Adversarial vulnerability means the model is broken, brittle, or getting confused by random noise."

## Falsifying Case
In high dimensions ($d \approx 10^6$), setting $\delta = \epsilon \cdot \text{sign}(w)$ yields an activation change $\Delta f = \epsilon \|w\|_1$. Even with an imperceptible per-pixel perturbation budget ($\epsilon \le 0.007$), the linear accumulation across thousands of dimensions produces a massive score change that easily crosses the decision threshold.
