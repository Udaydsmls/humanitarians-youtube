# Carry-Out

## The Carry-Out Sentence
"When variance is infinite, the sample mean never converges — so evaluating a system on average loss measures a quantity that does not exist."

## Wrong Guess Defeated
"Any running average will eventually converge if you collect enough samples."

## Falsifying Case
In a Cauchy or heavy-tailed distribution lacking finite variance, adding the 1,001st sample can displace the running mean by as much as the first ten samples combined. The running average never stabilizes.
