## DOUBLE-CHECK LAW — corrections and rewrites applied

The source reel was **not parroted**. What changed, and why:

1. **Register rewritten end to end.** Vox-editorial narration ("The near miss is
   the point") became Pragmatist: method first, then the decision trigger, then a
   full beat on where it fails. Nothing was carried across as-is.
2. **Structure rebuilt, not re-timed.** 20 short vox beats became 14 ai-explainer
   beats on the Claude spine. The framework beat now precedes the worked example
   (it did not before), and the failure mode was promoted from a passing mention
   to two dedicated beats (B09, B10).
3. **Two archival stills removed.** The source used LIGO/Virgo press images at
   its beats 2 and 5 (Livingston aerial, GW+EM observatory map). Under REBUILD
   LAW those are placeholders, not visuals. The detector story is now a native
   animated Manim timeline (B03); the observatory map went with the follow-up
   campaign when B03 was trimmed. No image rights are relied on anywhere in
   this cut, and nothing in the reel is a lifted image.
4. **Version-fragile detail stripped.** The Gravity Spy public-launch date and
   the "thousands of volunteers" headline came out — neither is load-bearing for
   the method, and volunteer counts drift.
5. **The Paired Doves cause was cut, not hedged.** The source hedged it
   ("scientists went looking for the cause"); this cut simply stops at the
   discovery, since the physical explanation is only "possibly linked" to
   beamsplitter motion in the technical source.
6. **De-sensationalised.** "Nearly cost LIGO its three-detector localization" is
   gone; B03 states only what the sources state — the search could not register
   both detectors, so the first alert was single-detector.
7. **No unsourced number survives on screen.** A drafted illustrative
   confidence figure for B07 was cut at visual QC rather than captioned; see
   `FACTCHECK.md` § Rounding and wording decisions, item 6.
8. **Voice changed, facts unchanged.** The source used Kokoro `af_heart`, which
   this toolkit does not ship. This cut is `af_bella` (Bella), the hai persona's
   house voice. No factual content moved with the voice change.
